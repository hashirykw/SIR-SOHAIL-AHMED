Where the marks come from · 7081 assessment objectives

What changes from 7115 to 7081

  • New topics on technology in production (4.2) and sustainable production (4.3).
  • Digital communication added to People in business, and a formulas and ratios list you're expected to use.
  • More marks for application: AO2 rises to 30%. Paper 1 questions now have four parts, and every Paper 2 part (a) is applied to the case.
  • Same shape: two papers, 90 minutes and 80 marks each. 2026 candidates still sit 7115.
